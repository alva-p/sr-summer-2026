# Portfolio

This folder collects sanitized, public-facing material that demonstrates the quality of the research
process during SR Summer 2026, intended for people evaluating this work for Web3 security
opportunities, and for the final SR Summer retrospective.

## Contents

* [case-study-template.md](case-study-template.md), template for a sanitized write-up of a research
  effort (architecture mapping, invariants developed, testing approach, outcome) without revealing
  confidential details.
* [final-retrospective-template.md](final-retrospective-template.md), template for the campaign
  closing retrospective (Week 11 / August 31, see [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)).

## Status

Empty templates for now (Initial week). Case studies will be added as research sprints complete and
can be sanitized appropriately, see
[SECURITY_AND_DISCLOSURE.md](../SECURITY_AND_DISCLOSURE.md) for what "sanitized" means in practice.
