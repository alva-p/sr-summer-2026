# AI-Assisted Workflow

This folder documents how AI tools are used during SR Summer 2026 research — and, just as
importantly, how they **aren't**. This is the main evidence base for the **Best AI-Assisted
Workflow** SR Summer category.

## Contents

* [workflow.md](workflow.md) — the `Spec → Invariant → Adversary → PoC → Report` pipeline, stage by
  stage.
* [safe-use-policy.md](safe-use-policy.md) — what is and isn't safe to share with AI tools.
* [verification-checklist.md](verification-checklist.md) — how every AI output is checked by a human
  before it's trusted.
* [prompt-library.md](prompt-library.md) — generic, reusable prompts (no target-specific content).
* [evaluation.md](evaluation.md) — running log of what worked, what didn't, and AI errors caught.

## Core principle

> AI accelerates **understanding, brainstorming, and review**. It never replaces **manual
> verification, running code, or reading the actual contracts**. Every claim in a report must be
> independently verified by a human before submission.
