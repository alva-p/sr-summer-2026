# AI Workflow Evaluation Log

A running, honest log of how AI assistance performed during SR Summer 2026. Updated weekly (see
[methodology/weekly-retrospective-template.md](../methodology/weekly-retrospective-template.md)).
This is the raw material for the eventual public AI-workflow writeup (Week 10) and the final
retrospective (Week 11 / closing).

The goal is **not** to make AI look good. It's to honestly track where it helped, where it didn't,
and where it was actively wrong, so the final writeup is credible.

## Format

For each week, record:

* **Useful interactions**: brief, generic description of what AI helped with and why it was useful.
* **Rejected outputs**: AI suggestions that were not used, and why.
* **Errors detected**: cases where AI was confidently wrong, and how the error was caught.
* **Net assessment**: did AI save time this week, cost time, or roughly break even?
